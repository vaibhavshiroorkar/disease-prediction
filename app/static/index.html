A little context matters

Every model has
its limits.

The symptom model learned from just 304 distinct patterns. The clinical models cover specific populations. The dengue model estimates severity after confirmation, and the malaria model reads a cropped cell, not a whole slide.

Missing optional measurements are filled with training medians and listed in the result. An input's effect on a score describes the model's behavior, not a medical cause.
